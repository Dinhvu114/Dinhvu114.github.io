lifecycle

I designed the dispatch mission state machine: 8 states, every transition checked on the backend and pushed in real time to dispatchers and drivers.

  1. 01CREATED
  2. 02DISPATCHED
  3. 03ACCEPTED
  4. 04EN_ROUTE
  5. 05ARRIVED_SCENE
  6. 06TRANSPORTING
  7. 07ARRIVED_HOSPITAL
  8. 08COMPLETED
